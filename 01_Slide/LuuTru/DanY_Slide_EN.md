# Slide outline (English) – about 12–15 slides, 10–15 minutes

1. **Title** – Application of GPR in Surveying Underground Cables and Utilities; group name; supervisor; HCMUS – Department of Nuclear Physics
2. **Motivation** – dense urban utilities in Ho Chi Minh City; outdated as-built drawings; excavation damage
3. **Objectives and approach** – GPR as the main method, RD7000 electromagnetic locator for verification
4. **Literature review** – international work (Lai 2018; Metje 2007; Royal 2011) and Vietnamese work (GPRTVN, HCMUS Geophysics group)
5. **NDT methods compared** – table: GPR, EM locator, magnetometer, ERT, acoustic
6. **GPR principle** – EM pulse and reflection; v = c/√εr; d = v·t/2; analogy with ultrasonic pulse-echo
7. **Hyperbola and velocity analysis** – t(x) equation; figure of hyperbolas for three velocities
8. **EM induction principle (RD7000)** – B = μ0I/(2πr); peak and null response; passive and active modes
9. **Survey workflow** – 5-step flowchart
10. **Simulation results (gprMax)** – model geometry and B-scan; metal vs. PVC pipe polarity
11. **Field survey** – site map, equipment, survey lines
12. **Field results** – processed radargram; RD7000 traces; comparison table
13. **Discussion** – accuracy, limitations (clay soil, adjacent cables), complementarity
14. **Conclusions and future work**
15. **References and Q&A**

Tips: one idea per slide; large figures; SI units; cite sources on each slide (small font); rehearse the English terms
(radargram, two-way travel time, permittivity, dewow, background removal, migration).
